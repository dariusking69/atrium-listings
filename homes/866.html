Welcome to Uptown Place Condos — Premier Downtown Orlando Living!

Experience comfort and convenience in the highly sought-after community of Uptown Place Condos. This charming residence offers a functional open layout with modern finishes and a warm, inviting atmosphere that feels like home the moment you walk in.

The interior features granite countertops, wood laminate flooring, decorative tile in the bathroom, and elegant crown molding throughout. The kitchen has been recently upgraded with a brand-new stainless steel appliance package, including a refrigerator, microwave, stove, and dishwasher, providing both style and functionality. Ample cabinetry and a breakfast bar make the kitchen perfect for everyday living or entertaining guests. The spacious living area offers great natural light and flexible space for relaxing or working from home.

Residents of Uptown Place enjoy resort-style amenities including a gorgeous community pool, fitness center, recreation room with TV and pool table, and beautifully maintained common areas. The property also offers assigned garage parking for added convenience and security.
