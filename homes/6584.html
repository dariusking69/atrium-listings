About this home

Welcome to Carmel by the Lake, a gated waterfront community offering the relaxed Florida lifestyle you’ve been looking for. This spacious fully furnished 5th-floor, 2-bedroom, 2-bath residence offers 1,615 square feet of comfortable living space, elevator access, and a private balcony overlooking the beautifully maintained community grounds. With furnishings already in place, this home offers the convenience of a move-in-ready living experience.
