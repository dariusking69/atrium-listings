About this home

Welcome to Cove at Pearl Lake, a beautifully situated second-floor 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom condo offering comfortable Florida living with peaceful views of Pearl Lake.

This bright and inviting condo features hardwood flooring in the common areas and carpet in both bedrooms, spacious living and dining areas, and plenty of natural light. The open kitchen connects seamlessly to the main living space, creating a comfortable layout for both everyday living and entertaining. A washer and dryer are included in the unit for added convenience.

The primary bedroom offers a walk-in closet and a spacious en-suite bathroom featuring a dual-sink vanity and walk-in shower. The second bedroom provides plenty of space for guests, a home office, or additional living needs.

The Best Part? The View! Step outside onto your private balcony and take in beautiful views of Pearl Lake and Florida's lush surroundings. Whether you're enjoying your morning coffee or unwinding after a long day, the peaceful waterfront setting offers a relaxing place to call home.

Enjoy easy access to I-4 and SR-436, making commuting throughout Central Florida convenient. The condo is also close to Uptown Altamonte, Cranes Roost Park, Altamonte Mall, restaurants, shopping, grocery stores, and everyday conveniences.
